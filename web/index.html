    Play with the computer keyboard (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z / X = octave) or a MIDI keyboard. Drag from a port to a port to patch; click a cable to remove it. Shift-click cards or drag a box on the canvas to select; "Make assembly" turns the selection into one card (a selection inside an open assembly becomes a nested one; a collapsed card dropped into an open frame nests too). On an open assembly, "merge…" on an exposed input joins it with another jack of the same kind.
