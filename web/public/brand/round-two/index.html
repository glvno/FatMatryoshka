Wordmarks

All letters have the same cap height. F, M, and the footless 1 inside K are teal. The K’s diagonal strokes stay in the regular text color.

Pictorial logos

These explore wider shells, shorter leads, simpler shapes, and different drawing styles. Each note identifies anything still needing correction.

How these were made

I used the design-partner skill: vary one or two constraints, compare the resulting concepts, then test them at their intended size. The wordmarks are editable SVG outlines. The pictorial studies were made with the built-in image generator.

Check the name first at 175 pixels, then the doll at 96 pixels. A direction can be worth keeping even if its cables still need to be redrawn.
